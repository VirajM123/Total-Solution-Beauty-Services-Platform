# Checkout payment contract (proposal)

The latest approved flow is Cart → Date/Time → Address → Booking Summary → Payment Method → Booking Confirmation. Customers may choose UPI or credit/debit card for online payment now, or Cash on Service for payment after completion. This replaces the earlier after-service-only policy.

## Current development behavior

The Customer App checkout is a local preview. It makes no booking or payment API request, creates no database record, charges no money, and issues no booking ID. The confirmation is prominently marked as a sample. Its payment status stays `pending`, including when UPI or card is selected, because no online transaction occurred. The cart remains intact. The UI uses the existing service prices, approved discount, and ₹50 convenience fee without adding fees.

The two service thumbnails used by the Booking Summary are saved under `assets/images/booking/` from the app's full-size service photos. The success check and confetti are Flutter UI shapes.

## Proposed production contract — requires backend and payment approval

| Field | Expected source and stable values |
| --- | --- |
| `booking_id` | Backend generated after a successful, idempotent booking creation. Never generated by the client. |
| `booking_status` | `pending_assignment`, `confirmed`, `assigned`, `in_progress`, `completed`, `cancelled`. |
| `payment_status` | `pending`, `paid`, `failed`, `refunded` where applicable. Separate from booking status. |
| `payment_method` | `upi`, `card`, `cash_on_service`. |
| `payment_timing` | `now` for UPI/card; `after_service` for Cash on Service. |
| `services` | Service IDs, quantities, and backend-validated unit prices. |
| `appointment` | Backend-validated date, time slot, and time zone. |
| `address_id` | Saved address validated for customer ownership and service coverage. |
| `amounts` | Backend-calculated subtotal, discount, convenience fee, grand total, paid amount, and outstanding balance in minor currency units. |
| `idempotency_key` | Unique key per intended booking, reused on retries. |
| `payment_reference` | Provider order/transaction identifiers for online payment; issued and verified by the backend/provider. |

For UPI/card, the backend must create the payment order and verify the provider's final result before showing `paid` or treating the online checkout as complete. A failed or abandoned payment cannot lead to a paid confirmation. The client must not trust a success callback alone. Cash on Service can create a confirmed booking with `payment_status: pending`; actual cash receipt must later be recorded once against the correct booking and balance by an authorized worker or collector, with an audit trail.

Booking completion and payment completion remain independent. A completed service may still have an outstanding cash balance. Customer-facing labels must be mapped from stable backend values, including Booking Confirmed, Finding Professional, Professional Assigned, Service In Progress, Service Completed, Payment Pending, and Payment Paid.

The backend must approve the API contract and provide authenticated booking/payment endpoints, server-side price and slot validation, idempotency, and real booking IDs before production checkout is enabled. Worker App cash collection, Admin reconciliation, and provider integration are outside this Customer App UI change.
